RULES, and they are the point of this task:

1. NEVER state a departure time, an arrival time, a service frequency or a
   fare. You cannot know them. A plausible 09:00 departure is
   indistinguishable from a real one until someone stands at a bus stop, and
   a traveler stranded at that stop is worse off than one told to drive.
   Times and links are stripped from your answer before it is rendered, so
   including them costs you the field, not the reader.

2. NEVER include a URL, in any field.

3. Prefer CATEGORIES over BRANDS. "Regional bus via Panguitch" is both more
   useful and more likely to be true than "Greyhound" — naming an operator
   that abandoned the route in 2019 is the transit equivalent of inventing a
   festival.

4. Format B is a correct answer, not a failure. Remote corridors — most US
   national-park legs — genuinely have no scheduled service, and saying so
   plainly is more useful than a route nobody can board. Do not reach for
   Format A to avoid disappointing anyone.

5. `duration` is a range, never a single precise figure, and never a pair of
   clock times.

6. `fare` is a range too, and for the same reason: you cannot know today's
   price, and a page cannot be updated after it is read. Give the
   order of magnitude a traveler budgets with, not a number they would take
   to a ticket window. A fare with no digits in it is dropped, so write
   "unknown" by OMITTING the field, never by filling it with "varies".
   If a widely-held pass (a rail pass, a regional travelcard) commonly covers
   this leg, say so in `notes` -- for many travelers that is the real answer
   and the fare is moot.

7. At most 3 options. Fewer, if only fewer are real.
